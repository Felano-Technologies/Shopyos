Delete your account

How to permanently delete your Shopyos account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Shopyos app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Select the Data tab.
  4. Under Danger Zone, tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Option 2 — Don't have the app anymore?

Email us from the address linked to your account and we'll process the request for you.

Email shopyosecommercehub@gmail.com

What happens after you request deletion

  • You're logged out of all devices immediately and can no longer sign in.
  • Your account is scheduled for permanent removal 7 days after the request, once any outstanding orders are settled.
  • You'll get an email and in-app confirmation when the request is received. Contact us before the 7 days are up if you change your mind.
Some records are kept longer where we're legally required to — order history for up to 3 years for accounting/tax purposes, and chat messages for 1 year after your last conversation. Everything else tied to your account is deleted.
